The Hand

i am sleeping
my father calls
You wake me up
fully enthralled

folks surrounding
call it astounding
but it’s not new
always been You

i am running through your
field of daisies
while biting the
hand that fed me

i can’t help but to
feel hypocritical when
i say that
oh that i love you

you stop my bleeding when
it’s all i know
i can’t escape your
light, oh, it glows

you tell me to arise
i struggle to
and it’s your hand that
gets me through

every blessing i cannot claim
the source is your name
my life feels like a game
but i don’t know the rules or how to play

despite this i can have peace
knowing i know the only
artist responsible for it
the Hand that guides me through it
