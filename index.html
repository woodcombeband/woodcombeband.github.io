About the Band

Hey, we're Woodcombe and we're from Houston, Texas.

The two braincells behind this project are Cru Niscavits and Alton Parker and we may not be the smartest but we do love Jesus (come to our church!)

Influenced by folk, ccm, midwest emo, bluegrass, and many other genres, Woodcombe has not pioneered a single thing but they don't care because they just want to make music.

We are also a part of Yesterday's Lunchbox
